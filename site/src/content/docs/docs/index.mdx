Delinya is a 2D drawing tool that runs in your web browser and works in
real units. Every position and size is in millimetres, so a drawing can be
built from exact numbers and printed at true scale. Woodwork was its first
use, but it suits any flat drawing where the sizes matter: templates,
layouts, panels, plans.

Open it at **[delinya.net](https://delinya.net)**. There is nothing to
install and no account; drawings are saved as files on your own computer.

## What it does

- **Shapes**: lines, rectangles (turned to any angle, with rounded
  corners), circles, paths of straight and curved segments, and text boxes.
- **Exact sizes**: type positions and sizes in the panel, or snap to the
  grid and to other shapes. Dimensions show sizes on the drawing.
- **Links**: make sizes equal through shared variables, and align or
  evenly space shapes, either once or as live links that follow later
  edits.
- **Layers** with their own line colours and see-through **fills** (light
  pastels, and dots, grid, hatching or crosshatch), and **line styles**:
  dashed, dotted and dash-dot lines in standard pen widths.
- **Printing** at full size or to scale, tiled across sheets with overlap
  guides, a check ruler and a title block; **export** to PDF, SVG and DXF.
- **Drawing together**: share a session link and edit the same drawing
  live with others.

## Browsers

Delinya works in current desktop browsers. In Chrome, Edge and other
Chromium browsers, **Save** writes straight back to the file you opened;
in Firefox and Safari, Save downloads a copy instead. On a touch screen a
finger pans and a pinch zooms, but a mouse or trackpad and a keyboard
suit exact work best.
