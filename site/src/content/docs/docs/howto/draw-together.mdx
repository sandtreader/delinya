---
title: Draw together
description: Share a drawing with someone else and work on it at the same time.
sidebar:
  order: 4
---

1. Open the drawing you want to share. Save it first, so you keep your
   own copy.
2. Click **Collaborate…** in the toolbar and check your **display name**.
3. Click **Start session**. The card shows the session link.
4. Copy the link (or show its **QR code** for someone on a phone or
   tablet) and send it to the people you're working with.
5. When they open it, Delinya joins them straight into the session with
   your drawing. Their names appear in the card's list, and their cursors
   on the drawing.

Everyone can now draw, move and edit; changes appear for everyone as
they're made. Each of you keeps your own zoom, selection and layer
visibility, and Undo takes back only your own changes.

To finish, each person clicks **Leave session**. The session doesn't keep
the drawing, so whoever needs it should **Save as…** before leaving.

See [Collaboration](/docs/guide/collaboration/) for the details.
