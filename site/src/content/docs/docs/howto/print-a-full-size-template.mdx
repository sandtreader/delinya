---
title: Print a full-size template
description: Print a drawing at 1:1 across several sheets and tape them together.
sidebar:
  order: 1
---

A full-size paper template is handy for marking out a curved part, a
cut-out or a set of holes. Delinya tiles a big drawing across ordinary
sheets.

1. Hide any layers you don't want on the template (notes, construction
   lines) with their layer cards. Hidden layers aren't printed.
2. Open **Print / Export…** (⌘P).
3. Choose your **Paper** (A4, say) and set **Scale** to **1:1 (full
   size)**. Leave **Orientation** on Auto to use the fewest sheets.
4. Decide whether you want **Dimensions** on the template, and turn on
   the **100 mm check ruler**.
5. Fill in the title block first if you like, in **Properties… →
   Drawing**: each sheet carries the title and its own tile label.
6. Click **Print…**. In the browser's print dialog, choose **actual size**
   (100%), not "fit to page".
7. Measure the **100 mm check ruler** on a printed sheet. If it isn't
   100 mm, the printer has scaled it: fix the print setting and print
   again.
8. Lay the sheets out by their labels: **A1, A2…** along the top row,
   **B1, B2…** below. Each overlaps the next by 10 mm, with a dashed guide
   where the next sheet's drawing starts. Line the edge of each sheet up
   with its neighbour's guide and tape them.

Prefer a file? **Download PDF** saves the same sheets to print later or
send to a print shop.
