Dimensions show a shape's size on the drawing: a rectangle's width and
height, a circle's diameter, a line's length. They're drawn in blue, on
screen and on paper, and they always show the shape's current size.

## Adding dimensions

- **Automatically**: with **Dimension new shapes** on (in the toolbar, on
  by default), a new rectangle, circle or line gets its dimensions as you
  draw it. Turn it off to draw without them. It's your own setting,
  remembered in your browser.
  <Screenshot src={toolbarButton} alt="Part of the toolbar: Redo, Rotate and Duplicate, then Dimension new shapes, pressed, then the line styles; its tooltip reads: Dimension new shapes: on. Drawing a rectangle, circle or line adds its dimensions" />
- **From the panel**: the **dimensions** row has a checkbox for each size
  the shape has; tick it to add that dimension, untick to remove it.
- **With the Dimension tool** (**D**): point at a rectangle's edge (top or
  bottom for its width, a side for its height), a circle's outline or a
  line, then click where the dimension should go. Or press, drag and
  release. The tool stays selected for the next one; Escape cancels.

<Screenshot src={placing} alt="The Dimension tool placing a rectangle's width: after a click on its top edge, the dimension, dashed and reading 100, follows the pointer above the rectangle" />

To measure between two points that aren't one shape's size, draw a line
between them (it snaps to both) and dimension its length.

## Moving and removing

With the Select tool, click a dimension to select it, then:

- **drag** it nearer to or further from the shape, or across the shape to
  the other side;
- press **Delete** to remove it.

<Screenshot src={selected} alt="A rectangle's height dimension, 50, selected in orange and dragged further out to the left; the width dimension above stays blue" />

## Radius labels

A rounded corner can carry a radius label, "R10", inside its curve. Click
a rounded corner with the Dimension tool to add or remove one, or tick
**label** beside the corner in the panel.

<ScreenshotRow>
  <Screenshot src={radiusAll} alt="A rectangle with all four corners rounded alike and one label, 4× R10" />
  <Screenshot src={radiusEach} alt="A rectangle with its corners set separately: R20 top left, R10 top right and bottom right, each labelled, and a sharp bottom-left corner" />
</ScreenshotRow>

A rectangle's **corner radii** have an **all** switch, on by default: one
radius and one label checkbox for all four corners. With all four corners
equal, a single label reads "4× R10". Switch **all** off to set each
corner on its own; switching it back on gives every corner the largest
radius. Radii too big for the rectangle are reduced to fit, and the panel
says so.

<Screenshot src={cornersPanel} alt="The rectangle's panel with all switched off under corner radii: top-left 20, top-right 10 and bottom-right 10 mm, each with label ticked, and bottom-left 0 with its label greyed out" />

## Showing and printing

**Show dimensions** in the status bar hides or shows dimensions and
radius labels on screen; it's your own setting. Printing and export have
their own **Dimensions** option.

Where a size is linked to a shared value, its dimension shows the
value's name: "= shelf". See [Links](/docs/guide/links/).
