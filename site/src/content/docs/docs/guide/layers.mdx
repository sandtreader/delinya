Every shape is on a layer. A new drawing has one; add more to keep parts
of a drawing apart: the parts, the holes, the cut lines, the notes.

## The layer palette

The layer palette floats below the tool palette, with one numbered
button per layer, **1** at the top. Layer 1 is drawn at the back.

- The highlighted button is the **current layer**: new shapes go on it.
- **+** adds a layer in front and makes it current.
- **Drag** a button up or down to reorder the layers.

Clicking a layer's button makes it current and opens its card (click
again, or press Escape, to close it):

- a **name**;
- a **line colour**, from a small palette of inks that print well;
- **hide** or **show**, and **lock** or **unlock**;
- **delete**, which deletes the layer and its shapes (asking first).

## Colour, hiding and locking

A layer's **colour** is part of the drawing: everyone sees it, and it
prints and exports in that colour.

**Hidden** and **locked** are your own view, not the drawing's: they
aren't saved, and in a shared session each person has their own. Opening
a drawing shows every layer, unlocked.

- A **hidden** layer isn't drawn, can't be selected or snapped to, and is
  left out of printing and export.
- A **locked** layer is drawn and snapped to, but its shapes can't be
  selected or changed. You can't draw on a hidden or locked current
  layer.

## Moving shapes between layers

With more than one layer, the selection panel has a **Layer** menu: pick
a layer to move the selected shapes onto it.

In DXF export, each layer becomes a DXF layer with its name and colour.
