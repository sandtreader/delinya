**Rotate…** and **Duplicate…** are toolbar buttons, after Undo and Redo.
Each opens a small card that acts on the selected shapes. The card stays
open while you work, so you can turn or copy again, or pick other shapes;
Escape or a click elsewhere closes it.

## Rotate

The Rotate card has:

- **Rotate by** an angle you type (15° to start with): clockwise, or
  anticlockwise if negative.
- Quarter turns: **Rotate 90° anticlockwise** and **Rotate 90° clockwise**.
- **Flip horizontally** (mirror left to right) and **Flip vertically**
  (mirror top to bottom).

<Screenshot src={rotateCard} alt="The Rotate card open under the toolbar's Rotate button: Rotate by 15°, then Rotate 90° anticlockwise and clockwise, Flip horizontally and Flip vertically" />

The selection turns about its own centre. You can also drag the
**rotate handle** above a selection (Shift for 15° steps), or type a
rectangle's or text box's **rotation** in the panel, which spins it about
its centre.

A turned rectangle keeps its own width, height and corners, and resizes
along its own sides.

<Screenshot src={turned} alt="A rectangle turned 30° clockwise, its width and height dimensions running along its own sides and still reading 100 and 50" />

## Duplicate

The Duplicate card sets:

- the **direction**: right, down, left or up;
- **×**, the number of copies (1 to 100);
- **gap**: the clear space between the selection and its copy, and
  between copies.

<Screenshot src={duplicateCard} alt="The Duplicate card open under the toolbar's Duplicate button: the Duplicate button, direction right (chosen), down, left and up, × 3, gap 20 mm, and link gaps, size and align all ticked" />

**Duplicate** makes the copies in a row and selects them, so pressing it
again carries the row on. **⌘D** (Ctrl+D) makes one more copy the same
way at any time.

### Linked copies

Three **link** checkboxes tie the row together:

- **gaps** (two or more copies): keeps the copies evenly spaced between
  the original and the last copy.
- **size**: links each copy's size to the original's through a shared
  variable, so resizing one resizes them all. The variables are named for
  the sizes ("width", "width 2"…); rename them in the shape panel or
  **Properties… → Variables**.
- **align**: keeps the copies lined up with the original (tops for a row
  across, left edges for a column down).

<Screenshot src={linkedRow} alt="A rectangle and three linked copies in a row: each width dimension reads 40 = width, = marks sit in the equal gaps, and a purple dashed guide runs along their tops" />

⌘D always makes a plain, unlinked copy.
