Several people can work on one drawing at once, each in their own
browser. Changes appear for everyone as they're made, and each person
sees the others' cursors.

<Screenshot src={cursor} alt="A drawing as Thomas Chippendale sees it in a session: a panel with two holes, and George Hepplewhite's cursor over it, a blue dot with his name beside it" />

## Starting a session

1. Click **Collaborate…** in the toolbar.
2. Check your **display name**: it's what others see beside your cursor.
3. The **Session ID** field already holds a new code. Click **Start
   session**.

<Screenshot src={startCard} alt="The Collaborate card open under its toolbar button: Display name Thomas Chippendale, a Session ID already filled in with a new code, and a Start session button" />

Your drawing becomes the session's drawing. The card then shows the
session's **link**, with buttons to copy it or show it as a **QR code**,
and the list of people in the session.

<Screenshot src={sessionCard} alt="The Collaborate card in a session: its delinya.net link with copy and QR code buttons, Connected, the users Thomas Chippendale (you) and George Hepplewhite, and Leave session; the toolbar button has a green dot" />

## Joining a session

- **Open a share link** (`https://delinya.net/s/abc-defg-hij`): Delinya
  opens and joins the session straight away.
- Or click **Collaborate…**, type or paste the session's code into
  **Session ID**, and click **Join session**.

Joining replaces your drawing with the session's. If you have unsaved
changes, Delinya asks first: **Save** (or **Save as…**) them, **Join
anyway**, or Cancel.

A drawing that comes from a session opens as Untitled, so Save can't
overwrite one of your own files with it. Save it under a name of your own
if you want to keep a copy.

## While in a session

- The Collaborate button shows a dot: green when connected, amber while
  connecting.
  <Screenshot src={button} alt="Part of the toolbar: the Collaborate button with a green dot, and its tooltip: Collaborate: share this drawing live with others (in a session with 1 other)" />
- Everything in the drawing is shared: shapes, links, layers and their
  colours, the title block, the grid spacing.
- Your view is your own: zoom and pan, the selection, hidden and locked
  layers, Show grid, Show dimensions and your line style.
- **Undo** undoes your own changes, not other people's.
- **New** or **Open** replaces the drawing for everyone, so Delinya asks
  first when others are in the session.

You can't yet see which shapes other people have selected; that's
planned.

## Leaving

Click **Leave session** in the card. Your copy of the drawing stays as it
was when you left.

The relay that passes changes between you doesn't store drawings: when
everyone has left, the session is gone, so save a copy first.
