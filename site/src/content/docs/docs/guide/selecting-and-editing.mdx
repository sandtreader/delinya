## Selecting

With the Select tool (**V**):

- **Click** a shape to select it; **Shift-click** to add it to the
  selection or take it out.
- **Drag on the background** to lasso: shapes wholly inside the rectangle
  are selected. Hold Shift to add to the selection. Escape cancels.
- **Click the background** to deselect, or press **Escape**.

Shapes on locked or hidden layers can't be selected.

## Moving and resizing

- **Drag** a selected shape to move it. Its own key points snap to other
  shapes' points and to the grid.
- A single selected shape has **handles**: a rectangle's corners, a
  circle's centre and radius, a line's ends. Drag one to resize.
- The **status bar** shows the position and size live as you move.
- For exact values, type them in the panel: see
  [Drawing shapes](/docs/guide/drawing-shapes/).

## Turning

A selection has a **rotate handle**, the circle on a stem above it. Drag
it to turn the selection about its centre in whole degrees, or 15° steps
with Shift. See [Rotate and duplicate](/docs/guide/rotate-and-duplicate/)
for exact angles and flips.

## Deleting and undoing

- **Delete** or **Backspace** deletes the selection, along with its
  dimensions. The panel has a **Delete** button too.
- **⌘Z** (Ctrl+Z) undoes, **⇧⌘Z** (Ctrl+Shift+Z, or Ctrl+Y) redoes, and
  the toolbar has Undo and Redo buttons. A whole drag or typed edit is one
  step.

## Fields

Values typed in the panel or a dialog apply when you press Enter or Tab,
or click elsewhere. **Escape** in a field puts back the value it had.

## Pan and zoom

- **Scroll** or **pinch** to zoom at the pointer.
- Hold **Space** and drag, or drag with the **middle mouse button**, to
  pan. On a touch screen, drag the background with a finger.
- **F**, or **Fit to drawing** in the status bar, shows the whole drawing.
- **Reset view** in the status bar goes back to the starting zoom and
  sends the floating palettes home.
