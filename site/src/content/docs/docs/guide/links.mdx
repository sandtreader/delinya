Links keep shapes in step: equal sizes, aligned edges, even spacing. You
can make each arrangement once, or keep it as a live link that follows
later edits.

## Arranging a selection

Select two or more shapes with the Select tool. The **first one you
selected is the reference** (drawn with a halo); the others move to it.
The panel then offers:

- **Align**: an **Across** row (left edges, centres, right edges) and a
  **Down** row (top edges, middles, bottom edges).
- **Centre**: **On reference**, centring the others on it both ways.
- **Space** (three or more shapes): **Gaps ↔** and **Gaps ↕** make the
  gaps equal, **Centres ↔** and **Centres ↕** make the centre-to-centre
  pitch equal, between the outermost two.
- **Equal** (shapes of one kind): give them the reference's width or
  height, diameter, or length.

## Once, or kept linked

With **Keep linked** off (the default), each action moves or resizes the
shapes once and leaves no link. Turn it on and the same actions create
**live links**:

- an aligned shape stays aligned when the reference moves;
- spaced shapes stay evenly spaced between the outer two;
- equal sizes go through a shared **variable**, so changing any of them
  changes them all.

Live links show on the drawing in purple: dashed guides along aligned
edges, "=" marks across equal gaps, and "= name" after a linked size's
dimension. A link that can't apply (it clashes with another, or goes
round in a circle) shows in red.

## Variables

Shared sizes are named variables. **Properties… → Variables** lists them
with their values; editing one changes every size linked to it. A linked
size in the panel has a badge showing its variable, and an unlink button.
Rename a variable (say Duplicate's "width 2" to "rail") by typing over its
name, there or in the badge; its links stay as they are.

## Removing links

- Click a link's guide, or a "= name" on a dimension, to get a small
  broken-chain button that removes that link.
- With the shapes selected, each link joining them shows a broken-chain
  button.
- A shape's panel lists the spacing and alignment links it belongs to,
  with a button to take it out of each.

Removing a link leaves everything where it is.

Duplicating can make links too: see
[Rotate and duplicate](/docs/guide/rotate-and-duplicate/).
