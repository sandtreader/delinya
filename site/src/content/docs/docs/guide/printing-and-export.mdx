## Settings

- **Paper**: A4 to A0, Letter, Legal or Tabloid.
- **Orientation**: portrait, landscape, or auto, which picks whichever
  needs fewer sheets.
- **Scale**: 2:1, 1:1, 1:2, 1:5, 1:10, 1:20, 1:50 or 1:100. **Fit** picks
  the largest that fits on one sheet.
- **Dimensions**: include dimensions and radius labels, or not.
- **Shape names**: include the names shapes show (see
  [Drawing shapes](/docs/guide/drawing-shapes/#names)), or not; on unless
  you turn it off.
- **Title block**: on unless you turn it off.
- **100 mm check ruler**: off unless you turn it on.

Paper, orientation and the checkboxes are remembered in your browser.

## The sheets

A sheet has:

- the drawing, at the scale chosen;
- a **title block** with the drawing's title, author, revision, scale,
  sheet and notes (set in **Properties… → Drawing**; the title falls back
  to the file name);
- a **100 mm check ruler**, if you turn it on: measure it on the printout
  to be sure your printer kept the scale.

The title block and ruler share a band along the bottom of the sheet.
With both off the band goes, leaving more room for the drawing.

<Screenshot src={footer} alt="The band along the bottom of a sheet, drawn large: on the left the 100 mm check ruler, marked 0, 50 and 100 mm, with Check: should measure 100 mm; on the right the title block: Title Bookcase side, Author Thomas Chippendale, Rev B, Scale 1:5, Sheet 1 of 1, and the notes; Delinya small beneath it" />

A drawing bigger than one sheet is **tiled** across several, labelled
A1, A2… across and B1, B2… down at the foot of each sheet. Neighbouring
tiles overlap by 10 mm, with dashed guides where the next one starts, so
you can line them up and tape them together.

The title block and check ruler go on the **last sheet** only, bottom
right, which is where the title block sits on the taped-up drawing; there
it carries that sheet's label. Sheets above the bottom row use their full
height for the drawing; the bottom row leaves the band clear.

<Screenshot src={tiled} alt="The bookcase side at 1:1 on A3, tiled across four sheets, two across and two down; dashed guides near the right and bottom edges of the sheets mark where the next ones start; A1, A2 and B1 are labelled small at their feet, and the title block is on the last sheet, B2, bottom right" />

Line widths print at their pen width whatever the scale, and layers print
in their colours, with their [fills](/docs/guide/layers/) (patterns, like
line widths, the same size on paper at any scale). Layers you've hidden
are left out.

## Printing and downloads

- **Print…** prints the sheets through your browser. Make sure the
  browser's print dialog isn't set to "fit to page", or the scale will be
  wrong: turn on the check ruler to be sure.
- **Download PDF** saves the same sheets as a PDF.
- **Download SVG** saves the whole drawing at 1:1, in millimetres, as one
  image, with dimensions and names as the settings say.
- **Download DXF** saves the shapes at 1:1, in millimetres, for CAD, CAM,
  laser cutters and CNC. See
  [Export for a laser cutter or CNC](/docs/howto/export-for-laser-or-cnc/).
